# CareVoice 🩺

**Understand Your Discharge. Follow Your Recovery.**

CareVoice is an AI-powered healthcare companion designed to help patients understand hospital discharge instructions and follow their recovery plan with confidence.

## 🚀 Live Demo

**[Open CareVoice](https://carevoice-f762.vercel.app)**

## 💡 Problem

Hospital discharge papers can be difficult to understand. Patients may struggle with:

* Complex medical terminology
* Medicine schedules
* Follow-up appointments
* Recovery instructions
* Remembering important tasks

## 💙 Solution

CareVoice converts complicated healthcare instructions into a simple, patient-friendly recovery experience.

### Key Features

* 📄 **Discharge Document Upload**

  * Upload hospital discharge papers as PDF or image.

* 🧠 **Simplified Healthcare Instructions**

  * Converts medical information into easier-to-understand instructions.

* 💊 **Medicine Tracking**

  * Helps patients understand and follow medicine schedules.

* 📅 **Appointment Tracking**

  * Keeps important follow-up appointments visible.

* 🔊 **Voice Assistant**

  * Designed for voice-based assistance in English, Hindi and Gujarati.

* 👨‍👩‍👧 **Caregiver Support**

  * Helps caregivers stay informed about the patient's recovery plan.

* 🔐 **User Authentication**

  * Secure login and demo login connected to the backend.
